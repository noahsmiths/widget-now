# Signed-out home

Mode: Persuade. The visitor should understand the product by seeing one public webpage compressed into a glanceable widget, then choose to create one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The transformation is the homepage. Refuse the conventional headline-plus-form hero and let a concrete before/after example explain Widget Now.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white stage, near-black interface ink, Widget Now blue for the action, and the source page’s mint, charcoal, and white inside the demonstration. Site-standard typography, slight object rotations, a cropped screenshot, a proportionally rendered iPhone home screen, and a curved ink arrow make the example feel casually assembled without changing the product chrome.

STORY: The visitor reads “Turn this…,” sees the supplied marketplace page, follows a rising, snaking arrow toward the phone, reads “Into this,” and sees the product name, current price, and last sold price distilled into a two-by-two widget on an iPhone home screen. A short statement names the repeated-checking problem and the live-widget outcome before the action opens authentication without leaving the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand header above a compact transformation stage positioned toward the top of the page. Desktop places a side-cropped, slightly tilted website capture left and a tilted 210px-wide iPhone right, connected by a hand-drawn curve; mobile stacks them and keeps the phone at 200px wide. Inside the phone, the price widget spans exactly two app columns and two app rows so its scale reads correctly against the surrounding icons. A wide, centered line of supporting copy sits between the comparison and the larger CTA.

FORM: Brief-pinned transformation tableau, first and only structure considered because the user specified its composition. Seed key: brief-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraint: The user explicitly owns the browser visual check; do not capture or inspect the implementation in a browser.
